# Phase 3A — customer ownership safeguards and planning checks

Implemented 4 September 2026. This is containment of audit F06, not the complete customer-service relationship architecture or a household collection planner.

## What changed

- Saving a citizen address retains the existing primary location's provider. It no longer chooses a new provider from nearby zones.
- Regulatory zone reassignment changes coverage responsibility only. It does not transfer customers, contracts, subscriptions or balances. The existing response field `rematched_customers` now returns zero.
- Company-created customers retain the explicitly chosen company. Coverage requires an active zone belonging to that company with a polygon covering the address. A nearby zone centre alone is not proof of coverage.
- Customer management shows a compact planning-check column in the existing searchable, paginated table. It explains missing coverage, inactive account/service/provider, or missing current matching agreement.
- Passing requires an active customer account and service, active provider, verified polygon coverage, and a current active/expiring contract linked to an active subscription and active compatible package within that company and service location.
- Citizen collection information is labelled area activity, not evidence of a pickup at their address. Foreign-company zone activity is excluded from the overview queries.

Passing these checks does not create a job, approve payment, prove a collection or enforce a debt policy.

## Files and existing endpoints

- Waste `src/repositories/customer-service.repository.ts`: ownership retention, tenant-scoped area activity and planning checks.
- Waste `src/repositories/waste.repository.ts`: remove automatic customer transfers from zone assignment.
- Auth `src/repositories/organization-user.repository.ts`: polygon-only matching for company-created customers.
- Web `src/app/dashboard/customer-service/page.tsx` and `dashboard/waste/page.tsx`: planning column and accurate explanations.
- Waste `scripts/customer-ownership-tests.cjs` and `scripts/test-stabilization.cjs`: rollback regression coverage.

Existing endpoints are retained: `GET/PUT /api/waste/customer-service/me`, `GET /api/waste/customers`, `PATCH /api/waste/zones/:id/service-assignment`, and `POST /api/auth/organization-customers`. No new endpoint, migration, automatic grant, reset or historical reassignment is introduced.

## Manual testing guide

Use development test accounts and disposable test records, not real customer agreements. Restart Auth and Waste services and refresh the frontend first. Keep different roles in separate browser profiles or existing isolated sessions.

1. Sign in as a company administrator and open Customer management. Confirm search, pagination, service status actions and the new Planning check column work. A customer without a current matching contract should show Needs review with a reason.
2. For an existing test customer, create/activate a contract using an active compatible package through Contracts & SLAs. Confirm the contract and subscription belong to this company and location. With active service/account and confirmed polygon coverage, the planning check should show Checks passed. A centre-only zone still needs coverage review; do not fabricate a boundary to bypass this check.
3. Suspend the test customer's service, refresh the directory, and confirm Needs review. Reactivate it and confirm the other checks determine readiness. A draft/suspended/expired or future-dated contract must not qualify.
4. Sign in as that customer and change the test address outside the provider's coverage. Save, then confirm the provider stays unchanged and the service zone clears. Refresh the original company's directory: the customer remains there and needs coverage review. A second company must not gain this customer.
5. As KCCA, reassign a disposable test zone to another company. Confirm the success message states customers and financial records were not transferred. The original company's customer remains visible there but needs coverage review. The citizen should not see the new company's zone activity.
6. As a fresh unassigned citizen, save an address. Confirm the provider remains Awaiting provider onboarding even when geographically inside a zone. Existing unassigned accounts need a future reviewed linking workflow: do not delete/recreate accounts or use duplicate-email onboarding as a workaround.
7. As a company administrator, create a new test customer using the existing onboarding form. An address inside this company's polygon can match; an outside address remains owned by the company but unmatched. Check normal success feedback and form reset still work.
8. Review the customer's existing contracts, subscriptions and balances before and after address/zone changes. They should remain unchanged. No payment or invoice should be generated by these changes.

Manual testing changes the selected test records. The automated suite below instead rolls back all its fixtures.
