# Urban Waste Management Operating System — product backbone

Kampala is the first deployment of a broader Urban Waste Management Operating System. Product navigation and delivery decisions follow four simple environments.

## KCCA Control Centre

Permits → licensed collectors → divisions → zones → routes → service standards → compliance.

KCCA authorizes and monitors. It does not create collector accounts, manage private staff, or operate company finances.

## Waste Organization

Employees → trucks → drivers → supervisors → customers → schedules → daily operations → finance.

The collector operates its own complete business system and shares only authorized regulatory and collection evidence with KCCA.

## Field Operations

Driver app → GPS → route → job → proof of collection → tonnage → weighbridge → incident.

The field experience stays sequential and task-focused.

## Citizens and Businesses

Collection schedule → complaints → missed pickup → subscription → payments → illegal dumping report.

Customers see their service relationship rather than internal operational complexity.

## Commercial model

- **Core City Platform:** annual city licence and support.
- **Collector Platform:** subscription for licensed collection organizations.
- **Premium Operations Management:** optional independent HR, accounting, payroll, fleet maintenance, fuel, customer billing and CRM capabilities.

Every implementation phase must answer which environment owns the action, what the next step is, and what information may cross organizational boundaries.
